# Ranking Criteria — Daily AI Digest

Every news item and discussion MUST pass through this ranking before being placed in the digest. Scores determine section placement.

## Three Criteria (each 1-10, total max 30)

### 1. Impacto na Indústria (Industry Impact)
How much does this change the AI landscape?
- **10** — Fundamental shift (new paradigm, trillion-dollar move, major regulation)
- **7-9** — Significant event (major model release, IPO filing, big acquisition)
- **4-6** — Notable but incremental (product update, research paper, single-company news)
- **1-3** — Minor (rumor, opinion piece, niche announcement)

### 2. Utilidade Imediata (Immediate Utility)
Can the reader apply this TODAY?
- **10** — Actionable tutorial, tool, or technique that saves hours
- **7-9** — Practical tip, config change, workflow improvement
- **4-6** — Useful context or trend to be aware of
- **1-3** — Pure theory, speculation, or entertainment

### 3. Intrigante / Interessante (Intrigue)
How compelling or thought-provoking is it?
- **10** — Raises fundamental questions, sparks debate, unexpected twist
- **7-9** — Surprising data, contrarian take, fascinating use case
- **4-6** — Solid story, worth reading
- **1-3** — Expected, routine announcement

## Placement Rules

| Total Score | Section | Format |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| 23-30 | Análise (Deep Dive) | Expanded, 3-5 paragraphs + link |
| 15-22 | Radar (if notícia) or Pulso (if discussão) | Compact, 1-2 lines + link |
| < 15 | Excluded or bottom of section | Brief mention or skip |
| N/A (tutorial) | Aplicação Prática | Extensive recipe with "O que você ganha" |

## "Tipo" Classification

Every item gets one of two types:

- **notícia**: A factual report about a product launch, company event, regulation, research result, or market move. Goes to Radar or Análise.
- **discussão**: A community conversation, debate, opinion piece, user experience report, or social sentiment. Goes to Pulso da Comunidade or Análise.

The type column in the ranking table controls where each item lands. A high-scoring discussão (e.g., 27) goes to Análise (Deep Dive) alongside high-scoring notícias.
