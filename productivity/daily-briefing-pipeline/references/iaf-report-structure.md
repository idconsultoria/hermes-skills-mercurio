# IAF — Manhã Aumentada: Report Structure

This is the canonical report structure for the "IAF — Manhã Aumentada" (Inteligência Artificial Facilitada / Aumentada) daily briefing.

## Header
```
IAF — MANHÃ AUMENTADA
A dose diária de conhecimento do profissional aumentado por IA
[Date — e.g., 06/jun/2026]
```

## Sections (in order)

### 🔥 Hot Take do Dia
- 1-2 paragraphs, depending on content quality
- **NOT** bullet-point highlights — the hot take paragraph IS the highlight
- The most important story or insight of the day
- Opinionated: filter through a practitioner's lens
- Voice: confident, informed, slightly conversational
- Should make the reader feel they now understand the key thing happening

### 📰 Radar de Notícias
- 5-7+ brief news items
- Each: 1-2 lines max
- Title/headline + one-line context
- Covers what didn't make the Hot Take cut
- Quick scan format — easy to skim

### 🎯 Deep Dive (2-3)
- The 2-3 most significant stories, detailed
- Each: context → analysis → links for further reading
- Why this matters, what it means practically
- 2-4 paragraphs per story
- Include both the announcement AND critical reactions

### 💡 Aplicação Prática
- Real user cases from forums (Reddit, HN, X)
- Highlight what practitioners are actually DOING with AI
- Link to original discussions
- Focus on: tools, workflows, tips, workarounds
- "This person tried X and got Y results — here's how"

### ⚡ Pulso da Comunidade
- What the community is saying about AI today
- Reddit/HN/X sentiment snippets
- Interesting threads worth following
- Tools getting buzz, techniques being discussed
- Brief quotes with context, linked to source

### 📚 Fontes
- All sources cited throughout the report
- URLs organized by section
- Clean list for reference
